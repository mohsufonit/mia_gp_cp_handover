01

Full Page

P2 follows launchBUILT (an overview frame, not a component)
▾
Purpose

Shows the whole page top to bottom so the order of sections is visible. Page 1 holds rings 1 to 28; page 2 holds rings 29 to 54. Section breaks (banners and mini-sections between rows of rings) travel with the rings they introduce, so page 2 opens with Shop by Gemstones.

Core logic

Header, hero banner, trust strip, breadcrumb and title, quick filters, filters (left side on desktop), result count, rings with breaks after ring 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 and 24 (Recently viewed, Gifting banner, Mia Fiora, Store finder, Dhanteras and Diwali, Shop by Metal), page numbers, recommendations, FAQ, Ring guide, footer.

Same top and bottom; the grid shows Shop by Gemstones, Complete your ring stack, Karatage, Budget, Visit the store between the rings.

Data needed and what the team can change

The order and the position of each break (they are placed by "after ring N").

Where it goes

Not applicable.

Edge cases

A very long page on phones. Breaks must never leave a half-filled row of rings; today they are placed on rows of four, so with five rings per row (desktop request) the positions must be recalculated.

03

Hero Carousel

P1 ships with launchBUILT (content typed in; two video slides)
▾
Purpose

Six full-width banners. The behaviour rules:

Core logic
  1. The first slide shows on load.
  2. Picture slides move on by themselves every 5 seconds.
  3. Video slides play with the sound off, play twice, then move on.
  4. The banner stops moving while the pointer is over it.
  5. On phones, swiping changes the slide; arrows are there on desktop.
  6. If the visitor has asked their device to reduce motion, nothing moves by itself.
  7. Each slide: small label, two-line headline, one sub-line, a button, and a link behind the whole banner.
Data needed and what the team can change

Per slide: picture or video, small label, headline (two lines), sub-line, button text, link, start and end dates. Suggested limit: 3 to 6 slides.

Where it goes

Slides 1 and 2 go to the Rings page; slides 3 to 6 go to the product page of the ring shown.

Edge cases
  • Video slow or blocked (phones often block autoplay): the still picture shows instead.
  • Only one slide: hide the arrows.
  • Long headline or sub-line; image missing; text on a busy photo (contrast).
  • A slide whose offer has ended must drop out by date.
  • The sub-line "up to 100% off on making charges" and "40% off" are offers: each must match the live offer terms and be signed off.
  • There are no dots and no pause button. A moving banner needs a way to pause it for accessibility; this is a gap to design.
  • The prices written in slide text are typed, not read from the catalogue; they can go out of date.

06

Filters and Sort

P0 blocks launchSIMULATED (buttons respond, the list does not change)
▾
Purpose

Lets the shopper narrow the list and change its order: a left rail on desktop, a bottom bar with a sheet on a phone.

Core logic
  • Desktop: a rail on the left that stays in view while scrolling. Top to bottom: removable chips for what is selected (with "Clear all"), Sort, then the groups Occasion, Budget, Karatage, Metal, Stone, Style, Wearer, Offer, and the switch "Loved by customers".
  • Phone: a Filter and Sort bar pinned to the bottom of the screen. Filter opens a sheet with all groups and a "Show results" button; Sort opens a sheet with the six options.
  • Several options can be chosen inside one group. A selected option appears as a removable chip above the list.
  • Sort options: Bestsellers, New arrivals, Price low to high, Price high to low, Biggest discount, Top rated. Choosing one changes the label.

Rules to approve (not yet built)

  1. Options inside one group combine with "or"; different groups combine with "and".
  2. Counts next to options update as other filters change.
  3. Choosing a filter or sort returns the list to page 1.
  4. "Loved by customers" means Bestsellers sorted first.
Data needed and what the team can change

The groups, their options and order; the default sort; which filters start selected; which options show a count. Today "Everyday", "₹10 to 25K" and "Loved by customers" start selected while the list is not filtered, so the page says it is filtered when it is not. Decide: start with nothing selected.

Where it goes

Stays on the page. Selected filters and the sort should be kept in the web address so a shared link opens the same view.

Edge cases
  • A combination with no results (needs an empty-results design: message, "Clear filters", suggestions).
  • An option with a count of 0, for example "Silver (14)" while there is no silver ring in the list, or "Under ₹10K" while the cheapest ring is ₹15,900.
  • Counts that change when another filter is chosen.
  • Eleven filters at once (chips wrap onto many lines and push the list down).
  • Sort by price while prices change during the visit.
  • Page 2 then a filter change: back to page 1.
  • Back button and refresh keep the filters; share link opens with the same filters.
  • Phone: the Filter button shows how many filters are on; "Show results" shows how many rings it will show; the tap area is large enough.
  • Keyboard and screen reader: options announce whether they are on or off; the sheet closes with Escape.
  • Not yet built from the review: a "hide filters" option, and five rings per row when filters are hidden.
  • The phone Filter and Sort bar is hard to see against a white page (feedback to be designed).

08

Product Grid and Cards

P0 blocks launchBUILT (design), SIMULATED (data)
▾
Purpose

Rings in rows: 4 across on desktop with the filter rail, 2 across on phones. 28 rings per page. Each card shows, top to bottom:

Core logic
  1. Photo (several photos for 33 of 54 rings) with a ribbon, wishlist heart, compare icon, zoom button and rating pill.
  2. Price, the price before the making-charge offer (struck through) and the percentage saved.
  3. "Incl. of taxes" with an (i) button that opens the price breakdown.
  4. Name, up to two attribute tags (karat only when the name says it, "Diamond" when the name says it, then the metal).
  5. Add to bag, and "Only N left in stock" when stock is low.

Rules in the page today (to be replaced by real rules)

  • Ribbon "Bestseller" if the ring has more than 260 reviews; "New arrival" if fewer than 90.
  • The struck-through price is the price before the making-charge offer; the "% off" is only that saving.
  • The low-stock note appears only when stock is genuinely low.
  • Ratings and review counts are made-up numbers.
  • Photos move on by themselves once a card with several photos is on screen; arrows on desktop, swipe on phones.
Data needed and what the team can change

Ribbon rules and wording; the low-stock limit; which attribute tags show; rings per row (4 with filters, 5 without; 2 on phones); the order of rings.

Where it goes
  • Photo and name go to the ring's product page.
  • (i) opens the price breakdown (card 28), which links to the product page.
  • Add to bag opens the size sheet (card 10).
  • Wishlist: nothing is saved yet; it should go to login or the wishlist.
  • Compare: card 11. Zoom: card 09.
Edge cases
  • Sold out: no design exists (suggest greyed photo, "Sold out" ribbon, "Notify me", no Add to bag).
  • A ring with one photo or none (no placeholder designed).
  • A ring with no offer (no struck price, no % off). Every ring in the sample has an offer, so this has not been seen.
  • Very long name (two lines, then a cut). Six-digit prices such as ₹1,23,456 must still fit next to the % off, especially on phones.
  • A ring with no reviews yet: hide the rating pill.
  • Two ribbons that would both apply.
  • Photo that fails to load; slow connection.
  • Price changes while the page is open (the gold rate moves).
  • Hover exists only on desktop; on phones the icons are always visible.
  • The (i) popup must stay on screen at every card position.

Size Sheet and Mini-cart

P0 blocks launchSIMULATED (nothing enters a real bag)
▾
Purpose

Asks for a ring size before anything goes to the bag, then confirms the add with a mini-cart.

Core logic
  1. Add to bag first asks for a ring size (Indian sizes 6 to 21). "Add to bag" stays disabled until one is picked.
  2. "Not sure? Measure at home" scrolls to the FAQ; "Send me a free ring sizer instead" skips the size.

  4. "Add both to bag" (card 19) asks for the size of the first ring, then, about 1.4 seconds later, the second.
Data needed and what the team can change

The size range; the wording; whether the free ring sizer offer exists.

Where it goes

Checkout has no destination yet (placeholder). "Measure at home" goes to the FAQ on this page.

Edge cases

  • A size that is sold out (no design).
  • The mini-cart never closes by itself, and shows only the last ring added.
  • The mini-cart must not cover the phone Filter bar or the compare tray (offsets exist; keep them).
  • Closing the sheet without choosing; double tap on Add to bag.
  • Add both: one of the two rings unavailable; the second sheet opening by itself may surprise the visitor.
  • The free ring sizer is a service promise: confirm it exists and what the visitor receives.
Design notes from Page 1

Gift Finder

P0 blocks launchBUILT (rules are placeholders)
▾
Purpose

Asks three questions (who it is for, the occasion, the budget) and shows the 6 best matches.

Core logic

How the recommendation works, in plain steps:

  1. "Who" decides which product types suit the person (for example Partner: rings, pendants, earrings).
  2. "Occasion" moves some types higher (for example Raksha Bandhan: bracelets and chains).
  3. "Budget" keeps products inside the price range.
  4. Products that are out of stock or not marked as gifts are removed.
  5. What is left is ranked by closeness to budget, then fit to the occasion, then customer rating. The top 6 are shown.
  6. The same answers always give the same 6, except in Surprise Me.
  • Surprise Me fills any unanswered question at random.
  • Not Sure suggests one gift at a time by metal purity and style, with next and previous.
What the team can change
  • A table the Mia team edits: each answer maps to product types, price range, metal and purity.
  • Questions and answer options can be added, removed or reordered.
  • Price bands and the number of results (6 by default).
Where it goes
  • Each result opens Quick View.
  • "See all" goes to the matching category page.
  • Start Over returns to question 1.
Edge cases
  • Fewer than 6 matches: show what exists and title it "closest matches".
  • Nothing inside the budget: show the nearest, with a note.
  • Spin Again should change the order, or be renamed.
  • "Self" reads "yourself", not "your self".
  • Visitors who reduce motion skip the slot animation.
  • Going Back during the animation.

Quick View

P0 blocks launchSIMULATED
▾
Purpose

Opens from any product: choose options, check delivery, add a gift message, pick a delivery date, optional WhatsApp heads-up, add to bag.

Core logic

Options depend on the product:

  • Earrings: weight only.
  • Rings: size (with a size guide) and weight.
  • Bracelets: size and weight.
  • Necklaces: size (none for pendants) and weight.
  • Chains: size and weight.
  • Coins: no options.
What the team can change
  • Each product says which options it has, and only those appear.
  • Message length, delivery date rules and WhatsApp on or off.
Where it goes
  • Product name goes to the full product page.
  • Add To Bag goes to the bag.
Edge cases
  • An option with one value is chosen automatically.
  • A sold-out option is greyed out.
  • A ring size is never preselected.
  • The price changes with the option.
  • Pincode not serviceable, or the delivery service is down.
  • Earliest date depends on product and pincode. Some dates are blocked.
  • Message too long, or unsupported characters.
  • WhatsApp: invalid number and the recipient's consent.
  • A product with one photo: no arrows.
  • The product sells out while Quick View is open.
